# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in ToolAtlas a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and prioritize the safety and maintainability of the project. Do not publish private information, secrets, exploit details, or unreviewed claims about security behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to `eng7mi@gmail.com`. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
